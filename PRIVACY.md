# Sakshi Vani Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026

Sakshi Vani ("the app") is an offline-first Lutheran hymns and Bible study app. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your choices.

## Data we collect

### On your device (local only)
- Reading progress, prayer logs, favorites, and reflections
- App preferences (theme, TTS engine, reminders)
- Optional manual city for weather

This data stays on your device unless you sign in and enable cloud sync.

### When you sign in (optional)
If you create an account (email, Google, phone, or email code), we store your synced data in **Firebase** under your user ID:
- Favorites, reflections, and prayer logs you choose to sync

We use **Firebase Authentication** to manage your account. We do not sell your data.

### Analytics and crash reports
When Firebase is enabled, we collect anonymized usage events (e.g. song opened, quiz completed) and crash reports via **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to improve stability.

### Ads
The free tier may show **Google AdMob** ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers per [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can remove ads via the in-app purchase.

### Location
Weather uses coarse location only when you allow it, via **Open-Meteo** (no API key; location coordinates are sent to fetch forecast). You can use a manual city instead.

## Data retention and deletion

- **Reset app** clears local data on this device.
- **Delete account** permanently removes your Firebase Auth user and cloud data.
- Email OTP codes expire after 10 minutes and are not stored long-term.

## Children's privacy

The app is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository or contact the app maintainer.

## Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
